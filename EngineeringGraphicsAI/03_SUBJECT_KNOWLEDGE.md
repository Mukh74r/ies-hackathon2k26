# 03. SUBJECT KNOWLEDGE — Core Curriculum & Topics

The **EngineeringGraphicsAI** engine possesses full domain mastery across all 9 core modules of University Engineering Graphics & Technical Drawing:

---

## 1. PROJECTION OF POINTS
- Points in 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th Quadrants.
- Front View ($p'$), Top View ($p$), Side View ($p''$).
- Distance above/below HP, in front of/behind VP.

## 2. PROJECTION OF LINES
- Line parallel to both HP & VP.
- Line perpendicular to HP / parallel to VP (and vice versa).
- Line inclined to one plane & parallel to the other.
- Line inclined to both HP ($\theta$) & VP ($\phi$).
- Determination of True Length ($TL$) and True Inclinations ($\theta, \phi$).
- Determination of Apparent Inclinations ($\alpha, \beta$) and Apparent Lengths ($a'b', ab$).
- Traces of Lines: Horizontal Trace ($HT$) and Vertical Trace ($VT$).

## 3. PROJECTION OF PLANES
- Planes parallel to HP / VP.
- Planes perpendicular to HP / VP.
- Planes inclined to one reference plane and perpendicular to the other.
- Oblique Planes (inclined to both HP & VP).
- Surface inclination & Edge/Corner inclination methods.

## 4. PROJECTION OF SOLIDS
- Prisms, Pyramids, Cylinders, and Cones.
- Axis inclined to HP and parallel to VP (and vice versa).
- Axis inclined to both reference planes.
- Solids resting on base edge, corner, or generator.

## 5. SECTIONS OF SOLIDS
- Section Planes parallel to HP / VP.
- Section Planes inclined to HP / VP.
- True Shape of Section vs Sectional View (Elevation/Plan).

## 6. DEVELOPMENT OF SURFACES
- Parallel Line Method (Prisms, Cylinders).
- Radial Line Method (Pyramids, Cones).
- Development of cut/truncated solids.

## 7. ORTHOGRAPHIC PROJECTION
- Converting 3D pictorial views into 2D Front, Top, and Side Views.
- First Angle vs Third Angle Projection conventions.

## 8. ISOMETRIC PROJECTION
- Isometric Scale vs True Scale.
- Isometric View vs Isometric Projection.
- Isometric drawings of plane figures, solids, and truncated solids.

## 9. AUXILIARY PROJECTION
- Auxiliary Front Views (AFV) on Auxiliary Vertical Planes (AVP).
- Auxiliary Top Views (ATV) on Auxiliary Inclined Planes (AIP).
