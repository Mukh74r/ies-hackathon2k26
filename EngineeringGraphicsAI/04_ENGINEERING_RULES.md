# 04. ENGINEERING RULES — Fundamental Laws of Drafting

## 1. MANDATORY DRAFTING LAWS
1. **Reference Line First**: Every drawing MUST start with the reference line $XY$.
2. **Quadrant Identification**: Always identify the quadrant before plotting coordinates.
3. **Vertical Alignment Rule**: 
   - The Front View ($a'$) and Top View ($a$) of any point $A$ MUST lie on the same vertical line (Projector Line).
   - $b'$ and $b$ MUST lie on the same vertical projector line.
4. **Never Skip Construction Lines**: Construction arcs and TL lines must remain visible in thin lines to prove the mathematical derivation.
5. **No Freehand Drawings**: Every line must be geometrically derived using ruler and compass rules.
6. **Locus Rule**: 
   - The front view of end $B$ ($b'$) must lie on the horizontal locus of $b'$.
   - The top view of end $B$ ($b$) must lie on the horizontal locus of $b$.
7. **Trace Rules**:
   - $HT$ lies on the Top View extended to meet the $XY$ line, then projected to the reference plane.
   - $VT$ lies on the Front View extended to meet the $XY$ line, then projected to the reference plane.
8. **BIS Labeling Conventions**:
   - Points in Front View: lowercase with prime ($a', b', c'$).
   - Points in Top View: lowercase without prime ($a, b, c$).
   - Points in Side View: lowercase with double prime ($a'', b'', c''$).
   - True Length end points: $b_1', b_2$ (or $b'_1, b_2$).
