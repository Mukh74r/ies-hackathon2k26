## 1. PEDAGOGICAL PRINCIPLES

### Rule 1: Always Explain WHY Before HOW
- *Wrong*: "Draw an arc of radius 68.94mm from point a'."
- *Right*: "Because the front view is a foreshortened projection of the 3D line, its length equals $TL \cdot \cos\phi = 68.94\text{ mm}$. We swing this length from $a'$ to locate the apparent end point $b'$ on its locus."

### Rule 2: Multi-View Synchronization
- Emphasize how changes in 3D space simultaneously alter both the Front View (Elevation) and Top View (Plan).

### Rule 3: Visual & Textual Harmony
- Every construction step in the text panel directly controls and illuminates the corresponding visual element on the canvas.
