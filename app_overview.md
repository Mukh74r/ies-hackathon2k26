## 🎯 Executive Summary & Value Proposition

DeepHub AI is an **AI-powered EdTech, Engineering & Hardware Research Platform** engineered for educators, researchers, STEM instructors, and academic institutions.

2. **First-Class Indian Regional Language Ecosystem**: Supports **11 Indian Regional Languages** (English, Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia) so educators across state and central boards can generate localized content.
